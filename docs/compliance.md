# Internal audit documentation checks

Checked 11 October 2026 against the [IIA 2024 Global Internal Audit Standards](https://www.theiia.org/globalassets/site/standards/condensedglobalinternalauditstandards_2024january9_printable.pdf), effective 9 January 2025. These are professional standards for internal audit functions, not a statutory compliance certification. The code implements limited record checks; it does not assess the quality of audit judgment or full conformance.

| Rule | Record check | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IIA 13.3, page 50 | Flag an engagement without objectives or scope | Text presence does not prove appropriate scope, risk assessment or chief audit executive approval. |
| IIA 14.6, page 56 | Flag a workpaper without testing, evidence, conclusion or reviewer | A reference does not prove accessible, reliable or sufficient evidence. People assess repeatability, accuracy, completeness and retention. |
| IIA 15.2, page 57 | Flag overdue unverified actions without a management explanation, and implemented actions awaiting verification | The recorded due date is agreed by the business, not a statutory deadline. Follow-up depth and escalation remain audit judgments. |

The local workflow also prohibits preparer self-review, owner self-verification and closing an engagement before workpapers are reviewed and requests received. These are conservative local workflow rules, not a claim that the standards prescribe these exact database checks. Approval records identify the operator; deployment access controls must establish who may act as chief audit executive.

Management can implement an action after the engagement closes. Verification needs separately recorded auditor evidence. Closure of the engagement does not close its management actions. No finding or approval is silently created from an imported status.

Evidence remains at the recorded location. No files are downloaded. Agree storage permissions, retention, confidentiality and applicable local privacy obligations before storing real records. The owner connection is privileged; this free local version is not a multi-user authorization system. History is an operator log, not tamper-proof evidence. No notice is sent and no assurance opinion is generated automatically.
